are, so a credit here covers the pages too; the CC BY music also needs its line wherever

### Pending replacement

`models/gumshoe/gumshoe.glb` started as the WWII US soldier from
[Toon Soldiers WW2 Demo](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/characters/humanoids/toon-soldiers-ww2-demo-85702)
(Polygon Blacksmith, Unity Asset Store) and has been modified a lot since. The Asset
Store's EULA does not allow redistributing an asset's files, modified or not, so it is
to be replaced with a character that can be.

## Made for wgrender

MIT, like the rest of the repository ([LICENSE](../../LICENSE)); the third-party files
above keep their own licenses:

- `sprites/logo/*`: the Whirling Gizmo logo.
- `textures/blobshadow.png`: drawn in Krita.
- Generated by `tools/gen_*.py`: `models/sphere/sphere.glb` and
  `textures/tiles_normal.png` (`gen_material_assets.py`), `textures/tiles.png` and
  `textures/tiles_sheet_normal.png` (`gen_tiles.py`), `textures/noise.png`
  (`gen_noise.py`), `textures/flame.png` and `textures/particle.png`
  (`gen_particles.py`), `textures/ui_panel.png` (`gen_ui_panel.py`). The `.ktx`
  files are compressed copies of the matching `.png`.
